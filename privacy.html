Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026
Quick answer

DefendFam doesn't ask for an account to read this site, and we don't collect personal information directly. We use Google Tag Manager to load Google Analytics, so we can see how the site gets used, which pages people read, roughly where they come from, that kind of thing. If you click a link to a partner like Bitwarden or Aura, that link can also pass along a tracking code so the partner knows the visit came from us, that's standard for affiliate programs and happens on their site, not ours. Details below.

Information we collect

There's no account signup, no newsletter, and no contact form on this site right now, so we don't collect names, emails, or financial information through DefendFam itself. If you email us directly, we'll have your email address and whatever you choose to tell us in that message. That's it.

How it's collected

The only way information reaches us is if you send it, by emailing the address at the bottom of this page. We don't have a form on the site that submits data anywhere.

Cookies and tracking

DefendFam uses Google Tag Manager to load Google Analytics on this site. Google Analytics sets cookies to tell visitors apart and measure things like which pages get read, how people arrive here, and roughly how long they stay. We don't see or store that data ourselves, it lives in Google's systems, under Google's own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy), not this one. If we add anything beyond analytics, advertising or retargeting, for instance, this policy will be updated first to say so, and the "last updated" date above will move.

Analytics providers

Google Analytics (GA4), loaded through Google Tag Manager. It's the only analytics provider running on this site right now; if that changes, we'll name the new one here.

Affiliate tracking

Some of the products recommended in our guidance pages are affiliate links, marked inline where they appear and detailed on our Affiliate Disclosure page. Clicking one of those links passes a tracking code to the partner's site so they know the visit came from DefendFam. That tracking happens on the partner's site, under their privacy policy, not ours, once you leave defendfam.com.

Advertising and retargeting

We don't run display advertising or retargeting pixels on this site today.

How we use what we collect

The only data we hold is whatever you send us directly by email, and we use it only to respond to you.

Who it's shared with

We don't sell, rent, or share personal information with anyone. The only third parties involved are the affiliate partners named on our guidance pages, and only in the limited sense described above: a tracking code tells them a click came from DefendFam, nothing more.

How long we keep it

Emails you send us are kept as long as needed to respond and for our own records, then deleted. Ask and we'll delete yours sooner.

Your rights, including California

You can ask what we hold, ask us to delete it, or ask us to stop using it: email us and we'll act on it. Because DefendFam doesn't sell personal information, there's currently no "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" mechanism to opt out of. If that changes as the site grows, we'll add one and update this section.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request about your data: privacy@defendfam.com.

Changes to this policy

If what we collect changes, this page changes first, and the "last updated" date at the top will move. We won't quietly expand what we collect without saying so here.
