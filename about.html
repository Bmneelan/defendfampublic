About DefendFam

DefendFam is an independent cybersecurity guidance site for families. It explains the scams and identity threats targeting households, and lists free fixes before paid ones.

At a glance

Co-founded by Brad Neelan and Kent Kaufmann. Operated by Stratemai LLC, an Illinois company. We work from primary sources and list free options first wherever they exist. DefendFam may earn commissions on some paid tools; it does not earn commissions on free ones.

We built this to protect the people we love, and to help others do the same

We've seen what scams can do to people close to us: family and friends targeted by the same kinds of fraud described here. That was the moment this stopped being just an idea and became personal. DefendFam is a way to help the person being targeted, and the person standing beside them, worried, searching for answers, and trying to help.

Why DefendFam exists

This site is how we give back what we know: free guidance first, the free or cheapest fix before any paid one where one exists, and a paid suggestion only when we think it's warranted. DefendFam earns a commission on some of the tools linked here, disclosed on the pages where it applies, and we don't accept payment to change the order things appear in.

How the guidance is built

We write to help someone make a clear decision in a stressful moment. We aim to trace each statistic to a primary source, agencies like the FTC, the FBI's Internet Crime Complaint Center, and NCMEC, and research from organizations like Verizon and the FIDO Alliance, rather than secondhand blog posts repeating each other. When we quote a named expert, we quote published, linkable reporting. We revisit guides as new data comes out.

Guides generally start with the threat, then the fix. Free and government resources get a plain link. Paid tools, when they appear, come with the reason they might be worth it and the tradeoff.

How DefendFam makes money

We're as tired of endless subscriptions as anyone. Much of what a family needs to stay safer costs nothing: a credit freeze, a free password manager, two-step verification, and knowing what a scam looks like. So our goal is to help you solve the problem without spending money where that's possible.

Sometimes there is a real need for a paid tool, such as help restoring your identity after it has been stolen, or monitoring for several family members at once. When we think it's warranted, we'll say why, describe the tradeoff, and may link to it through an affiliate program.

DefendFam earns affiliate commissions when someone buys certain paid tools through links on this site. It does not earn commissions on free tools, government resources, or credit freezes. Commissions are not the basis for what a guide recommends or the order options appear in. Details are in the affiliate disclosure.

Information only

Everything on DefendFam is general information meant to help you make your own decisions. It is not legal, financial, or professional advice, and it can't cover every situation. We work to keep it accurate and current, but details change, so confirm anything important with the company or agency involved.

Our recommendations are intended to help people, not to bloat your computer or your budget: the aim is to get you safer with as few tools and subscriptions as possible.
