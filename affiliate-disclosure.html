Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: August 25, 2026
Quick answer

Some of the products we mention, things like password managers, identity monitoring, and parental-control apps, are linked through affiliate programs. If you click through and subscribe, DefendFam may earn a commission. You pay the same price either way, and the commission never decides what we recommend or how we rank it.

What an affiliate link is

When we recommend a product like Bitwarden, Aura, or a Norton/LifeLock plan, some of those links carry a code that tells the company the visit came from DefendFam. If you sign up, they pay us a commission. It costs you nothing extra, the price is identical to going there directly.

How you'll see it marked

Beyond this page, a short disclosure note appears directly next to the paid software recommendations on our Teens, Adults, and Elders guidance pages, right where those links actually are, not just buried in a footer.

What this never buys
  • The order a recommendation appears in. Free options are listed first, every time, whether or not DefendFam earns anything from what follows.
  • Whether something gets recommended at all. We'd still say a product isn't worth it even if it advertised with us, and we still recommend plenty of free tools and government resources that pay us nothing.
  • The review itself. What a product does, what it doesn't solve, and who actually needs it gets evaluated the same way regardless of commission.
Where this fits legally

This disclosure follows FTC guidance on endorsements and affiliate marketing (16 CFR Part 255): clear, near the recommendation, and in plain language, not buried in fine print.
